# 7-Day Video Plan — DroidGuide Notes

**Audience:** English-speaking Android beginners  
**Format:** vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 25–45 seconds  
**Style:** original phone screen recording + short voice-over + readable captions  
**Goal:** earn attention by solving a real problem; do not add CPA links until CPAGrip approves the exact website/media and the offer allows the traffic source.

## Repeatable video structure

- 0–2 sec: a clear question or relatable problem.
- 2–6 sec: show the Settings screen or the result you will explain.
- 6–30 sec: demonstrate 2–3 steps, slowly enough to follow.
- 30–38 sec: one caution or useful takeaway.
- Last 2 sec: “Follow for more practical Android guides.”

Use your own narration and recordings. Since you selected PC, capture a real Android phone if you have access to one, or use the Android Studio Emulator (https://developer.android.com/studio/run/emulator) and label the video as an emulator demo. The emulator can need substantial PC resources; Google lists 16 GB RAM and 16 GB disk space for the best experience. Hide notifications, account names, email addresses, phone numbers, private photos, and other personal data before recording.

## Day 1 — Review app permissions

**Hook:** “Want to check which apps can use your camera or microphone?”

**Voice-over:** “Open Settings, tap Apps, and choose an app. Open Permissions to see what it can access. Review location, camera, microphone, contacts, and photos. Keep access that the app genuinely needs, and limit anything that does not make sense. Menu names can vary by phone.”

**Shots:** Settings → Apps → select an app → Permissions. Use a non-sensitive demo app and do not show private information. On PC, record the emulator window with OBS Studio or a screen recorder.

**On-screen text:** “Settings → Apps → App name → Permissions”

**Caption:** “Check app access in your Android settings. The menu may look a little different on your phone.”

## Day 2 — Find battery usage

**Hook:** “Which app is using the most battery on your phone?”

**Voice-over:** “Open Settings and search for Battery usage. Check which apps used power during the period shown. Remember, an app you used for a long video call may naturally use more battery. Look for apps using a lot of power when you barely used them.”

**Shots:** Settings search → Battery → Battery usage. Blur notifications and account details.

**On-screen text:** “Check the usage before changing settings.”

**Caption:** “Start with your phone’s built-in battery screen before installing a battery or cleaner app.”

## Day 3 — Free up storage safely

**Hook:** “Phone storage almost full? Don’t delete random folders.”

**Voice-over:** “Open Settings and tap Storage. Review which categories use the most space. Check large downloads and videos first. Back up important files before deleting them. Never remove unknown system files just because they look unfamiliar.”

**Shots:** Settings → Storage → show category breakdown (use demo data if personal files may appear).

**On-screen text:** “Check → Back up → Review → Delete only what you know.”

**Caption:** “A careful storage review is safer than deleting files at random.”

## Day 4 — Clear cache vs clear storage

**Hook:** “Clear cache and clear storage are NOT the same thing.”

**Voice-over:** “Clear cache removes temporary files. An app may load some content again afterward. Clear storage or clear data can remove the app’s local information and reset it. Read the option carefully before tapping.”

**Shots:** App info → Storage (use a harmless app and do not press Clear storage/data on your own important app).

**On-screen text:** “Cache = temporary files. Storage/data = app information.”

**Caption:** “Know the difference before clearing app data. The wording can vary by device.”

## Day 5 — Quiet one noisy app

**Hook:** “One app keeps sending too many notifications?”

**Voice-over:** “Open Settings, then Notifications or Apps. Choose the app and review its notification settings. Turn off categories you do not need, while keeping alerts that are important to you. Some phones let you manage notification categories separately.”

**Shots:** Settings → Notifications → App notifications → select demo app.

**On-screen text:** “Keep useful alerts. Mute the noise.”

**Caption:** “Customize notifications instead of disabling everything.”

## Day 6 — Find your Android version

**Hook:** “Need to know which Android version your phone runs?”

**Voice-over:** “Open Settings, scroll to About phone, and look for Android version. On some devices, the information may be under Software information. This helps you find instructions that match your phone.”

**Shots:** Settings → About phone → Android version / Software information.

**On-screen text:** “Settings → About phone → Android version”

**Caption:** “Check your Android version before following device-specific tutorials.”

## Day 7 — Before installing an unfamiliar app

**Hook:** “Three checks before installing an app you have never used.”

**Voice-over:** “First, check the app’s developer and official store listing. Second, read recent reviews for patterns, not just one comment. Third, think about the permissions it requests and whether they fit its purpose. Avoid installing apps from suspicious pop-ups or unknown download pages.”

**Shots:** Demonstrate checking a known app’s Play Store listing; do not promote an unknown APK or copy another brand’s listing.

**On-screen text:** “Developer · Recent reviews · Permissions”

**Caption:** “Take a moment to check the developer, reviews, and permissions before installing.”

## Editing checklist

- [ ] Export vertical 1080×1920 (9:16).
- [ ] Keep captions large, high contrast, and away from UI buttons.
- [ ] Remove dead time; keep cuts natural.
- [ ] Use clear audio and a quiet recording space.
- [ ] Use only music/sounds whose license allows the intended use; keep the license record.
- [ ] Do not copy other creators’ videos, voiceovers, graphics, or thumbnails.
- [ ] Do not promise “100% battery fix”, “secret free premium app”, or other unsupported results.
- [ ] Add a plain description and a relevant guide link where platform rules allow.
- [ ] Keep a spreadsheet with publish date, platform, title, views, average watch time/retention, saves, profile visits, and website clicks.

## Weekly review

Compare topics using watch retention, saves, meaningful comments, and website visits—not views alone. Make follow-up videos for questions people genuinely ask. Publish consistently, but do not spam groups or post the same link repeatedly.
